We value your (“User,” “You,” or “Your”) privacy and recognize the sensitivity of your personal information.

We are committed to protecting your personal information and using it only as appropriate to provide you with the best possible service, products and opportunities, as described in this Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy explains the online information practices of embedtek Private Limited (collectively, “Company,” “embedtek,” “embedtek App,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), which hosts the website identified in the supplied policy and the mobile application embedtek (collectively, the “Application”). It explains what information we collect, what we do with it and the rights you may have with respect to the data.

When using the Application, this Privacy Policy should be read together with the Terms of Use. By accessing the Application, you acknowledge that the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use govern your use of the Application.

The controller of your personal information is embedtek. The Company may provide products and services through affiliated companies. Affiliates may act as a Data Controller for personal information processed, used and stored as part of services and activities conducted by an embedtek affiliate. Certain services and activities may be conducted by several affiliates.

Privacy and personal-data-protection principles vary between countries. When you access a third-party website or application, you are subject to that third party’s privacy policy. Review that policy to understand the practices applying to information maintained by the third-party service.

01

What information we collect and how it is used

We may collect and hold personal information related to you that is necessary for conducting our business, including delivering products, services or information, improving our services and fulfilling regulatory obligations. When we request information, we will explain whether providing it is a statutory or contractual requirement and the consequences of not providing it.

Applicability check: The following account, upload, location, payment, advertising and analytics statements come from the supplied policy and require confirmation against the actual Embedtek website and application.

User profile

If you register for an account, we may collect your email address, username and password. The supplied policy also references registration through Facebook or Google and states that only your name and email address are collected from those services.

User content

We may collect content you create, share or post to the Application, including account information, photos, image files and associated metadata. Content shared publicly may be downloadable by other users. Avoid uploading images with embedded location data and do not include financial account numbers, social-security numbers or other sensitive information in submissions.

Support and enquiries

We may collect information submitted when you contact customer support or otherwise contact the Company, including your email address, name, attachments and other information included in the communication.

Location information

Depending on device settings or permissions, the supplied policy states that location information may be collected using geolocation technology.

Financial data

The supplied policy states that Stripe processes financial transactions and that embedtek is not provided personal transaction data. It also refers to possible future use of Apple and Google if the Application becomes available through their stores.

Device and internet usage

If you download the Application, information may be collected from the device, including identifiers used to recognize hardware and operating-system characteristics.

Cookies and tracking

The supplied policy describes cookies used to provide features, measure activity, understand page use, improve services and remember preferences. It also refers to Google Analytics, third-party tracking, analytics vendors and advertising networks. Third-party practices are governed by their own privacy policies and are not covered by this policy.

The supplied policy states that some devices may offer a “Do Not Track” feature and that the Application does not respond to “Do Not Track” signals.

03

Information not covered by this policy

The supplied policy states that the Application may allow communication with other users and posting through message boards or similar features. It states that information provided through those features is not protected by this Privacy Policy and advises users to review the privacy practices of relevant third parties before sharing information.

Applicability check: Confirm whether user-to-user communication, public posting or message-board features exist before retaining this section.
04

Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as it is necessary and relevant for the business. Criteria used to determine retention periods include:

  • How long the information is needed to provide services and operate the business.
  • The type of personal information collected.
  • Whether a legal, contractual or similar obligation requires retention, including applicable laws, preservation orders, litigation or dispute requirements.
05

Data rights

Subject to applicable limitations, the supplied policy states that you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information held about you.
  • Correct personal information that is incorrect, out of date or incomplete.
  • Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Opt out of marketing communications and object when there is no legitimate reason to use or hold your information.
  • Request portability of your information by receiving a digital copy or asking for it to be provided to a third party.
  • Withdraw consent at any time when consent is the basis for handling your information.

The supplied policy states that personal information is not sold to third parties for their own business or commercial use. Requests should be made using the contact information below. Deletion may affect access to certain parts of the Application.

The supplied policy states that requests will be answered as soon as practicable and within one month of receipt, with a possible two-month extension in exceptional cases. It also states that proof of identity may be requested.

Legal review: Confirm that the stated rights, response periods and identity-verification process match the jurisdictions and operating procedures that apply to Embedtek.
06

Security

The supplied policy states that security standards and procedures are maintained to prevent unauthorized access, including commercially reasonable measures such as data encryption, firewalls and server authentication. It also states that staff and support providers are required to observe privacy standards and may be audited for compliance.

No security defence is impenetrable. The security of applications, servers or databases cannot be guaranteed, and information transmitted over the internet may be intercepted.

Operational confirmation: Encryption, firewall, server-authentication, staff-control and audit claims must be verified before publication.
07

International audience

The supplied policy states that the Application is hosted in the United States and that personal information collected through it is transferred to the United States. It explains that this transfer may not provide the same level of protection available in other jurisdictions and identifies a legitimate interest in using United States service providers.

It advises users not to use the Application if they do not agree to the transfer and processing of personal information in the United States or if doing so is illegal in their home country.

Hosting confirmation: The actual website, email, analytics, application and future form-provider hosting locations must be confirmed before retaining this wording.
08

Children’s privacy

The supplied policy states that personal information is not knowingly collected from children under 16 and that information identified as having been provided by a child under 16 will be deleted as soon as possible.

09

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy describes current policies and the stated commitment to privacy. The content or services of the Application may change, and the Privacy Policy may therefore be updated. Important updates will be posted at the policy’s published location.
