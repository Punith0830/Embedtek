# Embedtek Terms of Use — standalone review build

Status: Repository review draft

Integration: Not linked to the existing website

Indexing: Disabled with `noindex, nofollow`

Open `terms.html` to review the responsive Terms of Use page direction.

## Content basis

The page is based on the user-supplied Terms of Use dated 19 February 2026. The design groups the source into readable sections and marks unverified product, operational and legal statements as review notes.

## Confirmation required before integration

- Exact legal company name and registered postal address.
- Whether the terms cover the marketing website, an application or both.
- Whether accounts, identity checks, uploads, advertising and user-generated content exist.
- Minimum user age and service eligibility.
- Privacy Policy destination and change-notification process.
- Intellectual-property ownership and applicable jurisdictions.
- Liability cap amount and currency.
- Governing law, courts, arbitration body and arbitration seat.
- Whether Apple App Store and US DMCA clauses apply.
- Final legal-notice and copyright contact details.

The file is deliberately isolated and includes `noindex, nofollow`. No existing website page has been modified to link to it.
